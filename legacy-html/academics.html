ACADEMIC CONTINUUM

Stages of Learning

Every age milestone has tailored pedagogical methods and dedicated mentorship.

🌟

Pre-Primary (Pre-KG, LKG, UKG)

Play-way methodology, Montessori-inspired sensory development, phonetic reading, number play, and emotional bonding in colorful, safe classrooms.

Focus: Curiosity & Happiness
📚

Primary (Grades 1 – 5)

Strong language foundation in English & Tamil, mental math, environmental studies, digital literacy basics, handwriting mastery, and moral storytelling.

Focus: Foundational Fluency
🔬

Middle School (Grades 6 – 8)

Specialized science labs, algebraic thinking, historical enquiry, Tamil literary appreciation, coding fundamentals, and inter-house athletic leagues.

Focus: Analytical Thinking
🎯

High School (Grades 9 & 10)

Tamil Nadu Matriculation board syllabus with conceptual depth. Rigorous unit tests, term revisions, one-on-one remedial coaching, and 100% board distinction preparation.

Track Record: 100% Board Pass Rate
🎓

Higher Secondary (+1 & +2 Streams)

Two premier academic streams:
• Bio-Maths / CS-Maths: Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Computer Science, Mathematics.
• Commerce: Commerce, Accountancy, Economics, Computer Applications.

Includes NEET/JEE & CA Foundation Guidance

Daily Rhythm & Assessment

⏰
08:45 AM – Morning Assembly & Moral Reflection
Prayer, school motto affirmation, news reading in Tamil & English, thought for the day, and national anthem.
📖
09:15 AM – 12:45 PM: Core Academic Periods
Mathematics, Sciences, Languages, and Social Studies with interactive digital board demonstrations.
🥗
12:45 PM – 01:30 PM: Nutritious Lunch & Wellness Break
Hygienic dining under staff supervision, filtered RO water stations, and relaxed peer interaction.
🧪
01:30 PM – 03:45 PM: Practicals, Sports & Co-Curriculars
Hands-on lab experiments, computer programming, library reading, Bharatanatyam, and outdoor athletics.
📝
Continuous Diagnostic Assessment (CCE)
Monthly unit tests, mid-term examinations, quarterly reviews, and detailed parent-teacher progress dialogues.
