Admissions Process & Guidelines

Transparent, welcoming, and child-centered. Discover the 6 simple steps to enroll your child at Sree Hari Saradha Vidhyalaya Matriculation School.

STEP-BY-STEP ROADMAP

The 6-Step Admission Journey

We ensure a seamless, supportive onboarding experience for both parent and child.

01

Enquiry & Campus Walkthrough

Visit our campus on Dharapuram Main Road or submit an online enquiry form. Tour our smart classrooms, science labs, library, and playfields with an admissions counselor.

02

Application Form Submission

Obtain the school registration prospectus kit and submit the completed application form along with student birth certificate and previous report cards (if applicable).

03

Friendly Student Interaction

For Kindergarten: An informal play and observation session. For Grades 1 to 11: A gentle diagnostic assessment in English, Tamil, and Math to evaluate baseline readiness.

04

Document Verification

Verification of Birth Certificate, Aadhaar card copy, Community Certificate (for state scholarship records), Transfer Certificate (TC), and passport size photos.

05

Fee Remittance & Official Receipt

Transparent fee payment via online portal, UPI, demand draft, or bank transfer. Flexible term-based installments available for parents.

06

Confirmation & Welcome Orientation

Receive your child's official Admission Letter, school uniform measurements, syllabus books set, school calendar, and parent app portal credentials.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓
What is the student-to-teacher ratio at SHSVM?
We maintain an optimal ratio of 1:18 in kindergarten and 1:24 in primary and high school to guarantee personalized attention, individualized feedback, and mentorship for every learner.
🚌
Are school bus transport facilities available?
Yes, our modern fleet of 18 school buses covers all major routes in Tiruppur town and surrounding residential zones within a 25 km radius. All buses feature GPS live tracking, female security attendants, speed governors, and first-aid kits.
📝
What streams are offered for +1 and +2 Higher Secondary?
We offer two main tracks: Group 1 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics) or Group 2 (Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics) for Science/Engineering/Medical aspirants; and Group 3 (Commerce, Accountancy, Economics, Computer Applications) for Business/CA/Law aspirants.
💳
Can school fees be paid in term installments?
Yes, we provide flexible three-term payment schedules (Term 1 at admission in June, Term 2 in October, Term 3 in January). Merit-cum-means scholarships are also awarded to deserving students.
