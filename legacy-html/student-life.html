INTER-HOUSE SPIRIT

The Four School Houses

Named after India's sacred rivers, fostering healthy competition, leadership, and camaraderie.

🌊

Ganga House

Color: Blue
Motto: Purity of Thought & Action. Champions of quiz, literary debate, and academic bowl.

🔥

Yamuna House

Color: Red
Motto: Courage & Tenacity. Fierce competitors in athletics, football, and track events.

🌿

Kaveri House

Color: Green
Motto: Growth & Harmony. Pioneers in eco-initiatives, social service, and gardening.

⚡

Narmada House

Color: Yellow
Motto: Brilliance & Grace. Leaders in music, classical dance, drama, and fine arts.

Student Clubs & Societies

🤖
Robotics & AI Innovation Club
Hands-on micro-controllers, Arduino circuits, Python logic, and drone assembly under mentor guidance.
🎭
Bharatanatyam & Fine Arts Academy
Traditional South Indian classical dance, Carnatic vocal music, Thevaram chanting, and pencil sketch workshops.
🌱
Eco Green & Herbal Heritage Club
Campus tree planting, rainwater harvesting monitoring, organic herbal garden maintenance, and zero-plastic drives.
🗣️
Tamil Ilakkiya Mandram & English Debating Society
Thirukkural recitation contests, extempore speech, Model United Nations (MUN), and creative writing journals.
🎖️
Scouts, Guides & Junior Red Cross (JRC)
Outdoor camping, first-aid training, disaster management drills, and community health awareness programs.
